Diabetes Care Plan

This is Mikael Rinnetmäki's type 1 diabetes care plan. As the patient, I have created this care plan myself to help both me and healthcare professionals see the big picture of my diabetes care. The care plan describes my goals and wishes regarding my care and clarifies the roles and responsibilities of the different parties.

The care plan was last updated on 1 June 2026.

Care team

My diabetes care takes place mainly at the Tampere diabetes clinic. For other matters, I usually contact occupational healthcare first, currently provided by Terveystalo, and until the end of last year by Mehiläinen. When my current employment ends in October 2026, my occupational healthcare coverage ends as well. I have comprehensive accident insurance.

I know that I can see a diabetes nurse when needed. However, I don't know who my diabetes nurse currently would be. I also know that the diabetes clinic can provide the services of a nutritionist, a podiatrist and a psychologist when needed, but at the moment I don't feel that any of them are part of my care team.

Goals

My own goal is to get along with diabetes as well as possible, without feeling that it burdens or limits my life too much. I know I feel well when my blood glucose levels are on target, and that motivates me to take good enough care of myself. I also don't want worry about complications to limit my life.

I develop various services, such as this care plan, to support my own care. At the moment this is mainly a hobby, but Sensotrend, the startup I founded to commercialize these applications, is still active in, for example, EU projects. In Finland, the business has so far proven too challenging.

Developing these services, and also creating a market for them, takes up most of my free time and sometimes also time and energy that I could use for taking care of myself. On the other hand, developing the services is also rewarding and sometimes motivates me to take better care of myself.

Sensation in my feet is good, no particular risk. I don't see a podiatrist regularly.

Oral health

My dental care is now included in occupational healthcare at Terveystalo. Previously I went to Oral at my own expense. I have a checkup once a year and see a dental hygienist twice a year. Recently there hasn't been anything in particular to treat.

Self-care

My glucose sensor readings are in target range 70 % of the time and below target range 5.5 % of the time. There is a roller coaster from time to time, but glucose levels rarely climb above 14 mmol/l. I haven't experienced dangerous or, in my view, harmful hypoglycemia in many years.

I am fairly satisfied with the level of my care, considering my current work situation, my hobbies and my own goals. My current employment ends in October 2026, after which I may have the enthusiasm and time to invest more in my care.

I use the mylife Loop system with a Libre 3 Plus sensor. I am satisfied with my diabetes supplies. It's a pity that I can no longer see my glucose level on my smartwatch since I switched to an iPhone. Ordering supplies is also sometimes frustrating; a recurring subscription order would be better.

Lifestyle

Diet

I think I eat fairly healthily. Sometimes portions are a bit too large for my needs. I sometimes wonder about my protein intake, since I rarely eat meat, chicken or fish due to my wife's preferences. Mostly just vegetables. In the mornings I eat half a tub of quark.

Exercise

I play football, but games are infrequent (every week or two) and there is hardly any training. I try to go to the gym twice a week, but there are breaks. In everyday life I mostly walk and cycle, but on remote work days (about half of the days) I often don't move much.

Smoking

I have never smoked.

Alcohol use

In moderation, about once a month. Usually just one drink, never more than five.

Mood and coping

Sometimes it feels like, having now done this diabetes thing for over a quarter of a century, I could perhaps take a little vacation from it. But you can't, really. This is how it goes. Sometimes you can put a bit more effort into your care and sometimes take it a bit easier.

I have seen a psychologist a couple of times to get tools for coping in other areas of life. At the moment I'm coping just fine.

Fitness to drive

I don't have a car of my own; sometimes we borrow or rent one for a trip. I am careful, and there have been no problems.
